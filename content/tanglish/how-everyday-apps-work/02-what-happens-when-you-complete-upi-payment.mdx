## ₹500 payment: screen-la success varradhukku munnadi

Rahul tea kadai-la QR scan panni ₹500 pay pannuraar. App “Processing…” nu kaattudhu. Account-la debit notification vandhudhu. Kadai owner-ku credit confirm aagala.

**Rahul:** “Money poiducha? Naan marubadiyum pay pannalama?”  
**Divya:** “First transaction status and reference check pannuvom. Debit notification mattum final result illa.”

Indha moment-la payment “pending” aagalam. App-ku final response varalai-na, actual outcome-ai verify pannanum.

## Yaar enna pannuraanga?

| Participant | Main role |
|---|---|
| UPI app | Pay request create panni status kaattum |
| NPCI UPI network | Participant banks idaiyila payment messages route pannum |
| Rahul-oda bank | Authentication, available funds and debit-ai handle pannum |
| Kadai owner-oda bank | Recipient account credit and response-ai handle pannum |

**Important:** PIN verify and account balance check bank-side responsibilities. NPCI-ai ellathaiyum check panra “super bank” madhiri imagine panna vendam.

## Successful flow-ai follow pannunga

1. Rahul recipient name, amount check panni UPI PIN enter pannuraar.
2. App payment request-ai network vazhiya send pannudhu.
3. Rahul-oda bank authorization and debit process pannudhu.
4. Recipient bank credit process pannudhu; participants result-ai exchange pannuraanga.
5. App confirmed result and transaction reference-ai kaattudhu.

Indha steps oru **simplified learning model**. Specific app and bank implementation details vary aagalam.

## Response varama timeout aana?

Debit process aana piragum app-ku final response varaama pogalam. App “pending” nu kaattumbodhu rendu possibilities irukku: payment complete aagirukkalam; illa final-a fail/reverse aagalam. Screen-la uncertainty irukkaradhunaala result-ai guess panna koodadhu.

**Safe next step:** App transaction history-la status and reference check pannunga. Bank statement or official support-la verify pannunga. Same recipient-ku udane marubadiyum pay pannina duplicate payment risk irukku. Final outcome-ku applicable bank/NPCI dispute process follow pannunga.

## Team idhai epdi design pannum?

Support team “debit notification = success” nu solla koodadhu. Engineering team same request retry aana duplicate debit varama **idempotency** use pannalam. Reconciliation means participants-oda records compare panni final state kandupidikkaradhu. Customer-ku “checking status” nu honest-a kaattanum.

## Neenga paakradhu vs actual state

| App-la theriyradhu | Enna verify pannanum |
|---|---|
| PIN accepted | Authorization start aachaa? Final credit aachaa? |
| Pending | Transaction reference-ku final state enna? |
| Successful | Recipient credit confirm aachaa? |
| Failed/reversed | Debit return aachaa? Bank record enna solludhu? |

## Try pannunga

QR scan pannumbodhu wrong recipient name therinjaal Pay tap panna munnadi enna pannanum? **Answer:** Stop. Recipient name and amount check pannunga. Payment complete aanadhukku appuram recover pannuradhai vida, munnadi check pannuradhu safer.

## Ninaivil vechukka

- Debit notification and final success ore event illa.
- NPCI route pannum; banks account-side decisions handle pannum.
- Pending-na outcome unknown; status verify panni dhaan retry decide pannunga.
- Reference ID dispute and support-ku useful.

## Common questions

**Pending-na money lost-a?** Illai. Final status confirm aagatha state-nu artham. Official transaction history and bank record-la check pannunga.

**Timeout-na payment fail-a?** Avasiyam illa. Response lost aagirukkalam; bank processing complete aagirukkalam.
