## Road network-ai epdi think pannanum?

Map-ai connected roads-oda graph-nu nenachukkonga. Junctions connect aagum; road segment ovvondrukkum travel-time estimate irukkum. Traffic, road closure, turns, toll preference madhiri factors path-ai affect pannalam.

**Historical pattern:** Indha road weekday 6 pm-ku usually slow-a?  
**Live condition:** Innikku ippo unusual jam irukkaa?

Rendum combine pannina, only past or only present use pannuradhai vida better ETA estimate kidaikkum.

## Route choose panra flow

1. Start and destination enter pannunga.
2. Candidate roads/path identify pannum.
3. Road segments-ku estimated time assign pannum.
4. Suitable routes-oda total travel time compare pannum.
5. Preferred route and ETA kaattum.

Idhu Google-oda public explanation-ai base pannina beginner model. Exact proprietary algorithm-oda full blueprint illa.

Rahul halfway-la irukkumbodhu ahead accident report varudhu. Original route 35 minutes; alternate route 27 minutes nu estimate. Navigation new path suggest pannalam. Difference just 30 seconds-na repeated turning instructions driver-ku bother aagalam.

## Prediction wrong aana?

Accident clear aagalam; unexpected event varalam; phone location late-a update aagalam. ETA oru **estimate**, promise illa. Route recommendation new evidence vandha update aagum. Driving safety-ku phone-ai disturb pannama voice instruction and road signs follow pannunga.

## Ninaivil vechukka

- Shortest distance and fastest time different.
- Historical pattern + live condition ETA-ku help pannum.
- New traffic vandha alternative routes compare pannalam.
- ETA prediction dhaan; change aagalam.

**Oru faster road vandha udane reroute-a?** Every small difference-ku illa; route stability-um decision-la matter pannum.
